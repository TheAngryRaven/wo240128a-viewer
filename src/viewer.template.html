What the demo is running

The screen is a hand-rolled 1-bit renderer, no library: a 240 × 128 framebuffer with clip rectangle, fill / rect / invert / dither primitives, a 5 × 7 bitmap font, a parametric seven-segment font drawn from rectangles at any height, and a transform blit (scale + rotate by inverse mapping) used for the logo. Every animation is a tween in milliseconds, so the UI tick rate only changes smoothness, never timing. The loop is fixed-step: pick a rate above and the renderer runs exactly that many ticks per second. Written as the C it will become, with integer pixel math.

Why 30: a full frame is 3840 bytes, about 8 ms on the UC1608's SPI at 4 MHz and under 1 ms of CPU to draw, so the bus and the M33 are not the limit. The FSTN panel is: it needs roughly 150 ms to switch, so anything faster than ~20 updates per second only smears. The "FSTN response" toggle models that lag so a motion that looks right here looks right on glass. 15 fps is visibly steppy on the RPM bar; 60 buys nothing the glass can show.

Events: the session runs a simulated direct-drive kart lap at 4× speed with three sectors. A sector faster than the session best goes purple: strobe, 1-px shake, badge, sparkles, then the badge flies into its sector box. A lap end freezes and flashes the time, rolls the digits over, and a best lap gets a banner. Reboot replays the start-up: logo spin-in, name, fly-to-header, gauge sweep, dissolve.

How to read the model

Axes follow the drawing's front view: +X right, +Y up, +Z toward you, with the glass front face at Z = 0. The 23 signal pins, the LED pins and the two pegs are bent 90° and point to −Z, so the module lies flat on the PCB with the frame's rear face on it. VA is the clear window, AA is the live dot matrix.

Everything a PCB or bezel depends on comes straight off the drawing. These were scaled from it rather than dimensioned, so treat them as cosmetic:

  • Pin tab height on the ledge (2.6), A/K pin thickness (0.5), UC1608 outline (10.9 × 1.7), pull-tape length, end-cap width (6.4).
  • Signal-pin length is the drawing's 8.0 ± 0.5 measured from the LCD rear face, so the tips reach 10.9 from the front face; the 9.5 overall thickness is to the A/K pin tips.
  • The side-wall notch (10.0 MAX, 23.5 MIN from the top) is modelled on both sides, open toward the front.

In OpenSCAD, mode = "footprint" gives the hole pattern in the PCB plane and mode = "bezel" the window outlines; both export as DXF. origin can be the outline centre, the active-area centre, the corner, or pin 1.
